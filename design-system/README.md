# Yohji Yamamoto x HBS

Presentation system for the HBS IFC Japan team working with Yohji Yamamoto on US expansion. It follows the brand's minimalism: black and white, generous space, thin rules, no decoration.

## Principles

Black and white only: the palette has no accent color. Emphasis comes from size, weight, an italic word or one inverted `block`.

Space before structure: columns are separated by `space-column` and topped by a 1px rule. There are no card outlines, fills or shadows.

Square everything: `radius-none` applies to blocks and photos.

Few words per slide: one title, one number row or one set of columns, one takeaway.

## Color

Two grounds. Use `ground` in Black for the cover and slides that frame the work (scope, recommendations). Use `ground` in Paper for plans, timelines and dense slides. A deck alternates them; never mix both on one slide except the single inverted `block`.

Text hierarchy on any ground: `ink` for titles and lead sentences, `ink-soft` for body and lists, `ink-muted` for owners, footers and sources.

Rules: `rule-strong` above primary columns, `rule` above secondary ones and footers.

## Type

Archivo at weight 500 for titles, 400 for key numbers. Inter Tight for text. Both load from Google Fonts.

Sizes on a 1920x1080 slide: `cover-title` 120px, `slide-title` 60px, `key-number` 56px, `column-title` 32px, `lead` 26px, `body` 24px. Nothing under 24px.

Titles in sentence case with no final period. Emphasis in body text uses italic, never color.

## Layout

Margins: `margin-x` 128px left and right, `margin-top` 112px, 160px at the bottom when a 24px footer is pinned at 64px from the edge.

Typical slide: title, then a row of two to three key numbers under `rule`, then two or three columns under `rule-strong`, then a footer in `ink-muted`.

## Imagery

Team portraits are square, grayscale, 296px on the cover, with the name under each in `body`. Use no stock photos or icons.

## Copy

English for client and course material. Concrete names, dates and figures. Owners appear as "Leads: Name, Name". Sources and caveats go in the footer or speaker notes.

## Reference deck

"Yohji Yamamoto IFC scope and January plan", November 2026: cover with portraits, scope slide on Black, Tokyo plan on Paper.
