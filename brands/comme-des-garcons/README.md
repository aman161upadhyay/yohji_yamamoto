## US footprint
Facts below come from opened pages; dates are source dates.
- New York flagship at 520 West 22nd Street, Chelsea. Opened in 1999 and first refurbished in December 2013 in gold, reopening with Dover Street Market New York (Wallpaper, 2013). Closed one day on 5 August 2026 to reopen with the Fall/Winter 2026 collections (RETAILBOSS, 2026).
- CDG Pocket shop at 112 Tenth Avenue, Chelsea, opened late August 2015 (Time Out, Hypebeast, 2015). A 500 sq ft Pocket shop opened inside Bloomingdale's 59th Street in March 2021 (mr-mag, 2021).
- Los Angeles Guerilla Store, 125 West 4th Street, reported March 2008 (Hypebeast).
- Dover Street Market New York and Los Angeles carry CDG lines and CDG-specific drops. DSM itself is covered in another folder.
- Online: Joffe said in September 2015 that the main line was not sold online and that DSM e-shops sold only some items (Another Magazine). A dedicated CDG online shop shipping worldwide launched on 1 November 2018 with the CDG line (Hypebeast). Collaboration drops now also sell on the DSMNY E-Shop (DSM New York pages).
- Wholesale: Bloomingdale's (above) and online retailer SSENSE, which Joffe called successful in 2015. A Nordstrom men's shop in New York (2018) appears in a search result but the page would not open, so it is unverified.
- Scale clues: a BoF profile (page undated, probably about 2013, inference) cites about 220 million dollars a year, with Play at 12 percent of revenue, no outside investors and a growth model of many parallel brands. CNBC (August 2017, date from URL) reports the businesses Joffe oversees at over 280 million dollars a year.

## Three takeaways for Yohji
All three are our inferences from the facts in `initiatives.csv`.
1. Treat the New York store as a cultural venue with art-world neighbours. Reasoning: CDG placed a Meg Webster sculpture in its Chelsea store across from Dia (rows 04, 05), staged a Kawakubo installation at Independent (row 06) and showed at the Met (row 07), which builds standing at modest cost compared with advertising.
2. Test small formats and partner shop-in-shops before large stores. Reasoning: CDG used a Tenth Avenue Pocket shop and a 500 sq ft Bloomingdale's shop (rows 08, 09) to sell accessible items, so Yohji could trial a compact capsule space without a full flagship.
3. Release drops in New York first, then open them to online. Reasoning: the Jordan 1, A$AP Nast and Nike releases (rows 10, 12, 15) start in store and later move to e-shops and other cities, which gives fans a reason to visit and lets the brand collect online data later.
