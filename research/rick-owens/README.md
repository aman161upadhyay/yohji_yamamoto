# Rick Owens public-source research

Reviewed 11 November 2026.

- [Benchmark](benchmark.md): New York cases, wider-US precedents, global analogies, digital/service audit, audience hypotheses, and three analytical takeaways.
- [Source register](sources.csv): source links, classifications, review date, and limitations.

Only public-source evidence and original general analysis are included. Primary promotional claims, independent reporting, inference, and unverified outcomes are distinguished. Publicly available information is not necessarily audited evidence of commercial performance. Full articles, copyrighted images, private statements, and confidential documents are excluded.

This is a research contribution, not a complete census of every brand activity. Current service policies and retail listings should be rechecked before operational use.
