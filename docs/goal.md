# Goal of the brand benchmarks

## Context

For Yohji Yamamoto's US expansion, our workstream (brand and community) has to propose options to build Yohji's brand, community and digital positioning in the US, with New York as the priority.

## Goal

Learn from what comparable brands have actually done in the US. We benchmark initiatives, such as in-store events, recurring programming, omnichannel set-ups, pop-ups and residencies, digital and creator activity, collaborations and community mechanisms. Each brand is the source we mine them from.

## The question each brand should help answer

Which initiatives could Yohji run in New York to build a community and a digital presence, and what would it take?

## What you deliver per brand

- Its most relevant US initiatives, each classified in one family and backed by a source (rows in `brands/<brand-slug>/initiatives.csv`)
- Three takeaways for Yohji
- The gaps we still need to close

## How we use it

We compare initiatives across brands, keep the ones that fit Yohji's universe, and turn them into options.
