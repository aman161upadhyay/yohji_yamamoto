Four attributes describe an initiative and four tell us whether Yohji could run it

Describe

Objective

Why the brand ran it, from testing demand to driving sales

Audience

Who it targeted: fans, local creatives, new buyers or tourists

Format and cadence

How long it ran, how often and at what scale

Partners

Who took part and what each partner brought

Assess

Physical-digital bridge

What moves people between store and online

Investment

The figures when public, otherwise the size and length of the run

Results

The outcomes the brand reported and its next move

Transferability to Yohji

Fit, cost, feasibility and risk, rated from 1 to 3

Note: 3 means strong evidence or fit, 2 partial, 1 weak or not found. Source: team benchmark framework

9

Public data is thin, so each attribute accepts proxies. Investment uses store size and length of run when figures are not public. Results often rely on the brand’s next move, and a pop-up that led to a permanent store is a strong signal. The physical-digital bridge serves the sponsors’ digital positioning question.
