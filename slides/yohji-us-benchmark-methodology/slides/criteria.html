What we look for

Why it matters for Yohji

Brand proximity

Designer-led, sharp aesthetic, high prices, niche fan base

Lessons transfer only from brands that sell the same kind of desire

Comparable US situation

Japanese or avant-garde brand entering the US with few stores

Yohji is weighing a second store after Wooster, so early US moves give the most useful signal

Community mechanics

Pop-ups, residencies, collaborations, events, a store linked to online

The workstream covers community and digital, so we need initiatives we can observe

Visible results

Traction, longevity or a well-documented failure

A pop-up that became a permanent store, or a retreat, tells us more than an announcement

Transferability

Resources close to Yohji’s, meaning an independent house
